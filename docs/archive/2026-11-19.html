Falling orders are only half the story.

Manufacturers are also increasing output, while weaker consumer electronics sales could further depress demand. Strong demand for high-performance storage in the US continues to drive manufacturers to expand capacity and upgrade to newer process technologies. At the same time, the rising adoption of quad-level cell (QLC) technology and increasing number of layers in the NAND chips will further boost bit output, TrendForce observes.
