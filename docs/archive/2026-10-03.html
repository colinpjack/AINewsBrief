Like everything good and pure in this world, AI has ruined that, too, necessitating some strict new limits.

“arXiv now limits submitters to up to two submissions per calendar month, with a limit of three total active submissions at any given time,” arXiv community engagement manager Kat Boboris wrote in a Thursday announcement of the change, which was effective immediately. According to an arXiv spokesperson, an active paper is one that has been submitted, is under consideration and review, but not yet published on the site. It’s worth noting that large institutions whose projects involve multiple researchers are unlikely to see a significant impact on their submissions - this limit is per submitter, the announcement explains, so a research paper with multiple authors only counts toward the submission rate of the individual who files it.

Rejected papers count toward that monthly total. If that sounds like some strict limitation - it is, but it’s necessary, based on arXiv’s numbers. In September alone, arXiv said that it had received 40,363 submissions.
