Worse are the extended delivery times, with optical network kit that used to arrive within three months now taking 12 to 15, and some orders taking up to 18 months. This is as bad as, if not worse than, the shortages during the pandemic, when chip supply problems left network vendors unable to fulfill orders . The issue now is that because of the AI boom, demand is outpacing supplies.

In its report, "AI, Connected," RETN says developers building outside established datacenter hubs may need to involve network carriers two to five years before opening. The sudden demand for network kit from hyperscalers and AI campuses is only one factor, RETN says. Raw material shortages, skilled labor shortages, energy constraints, geopolitical tensions, and logistical challenges are also compounding the issue.

Global smartphone shipments are forecast to fall 14 percent this year and another 6 percent in 2027, with a recovery not expected until 2028. That's the outlook from Counterpoint Research's latest Smartphone Market Outlook Tracker , which points to rising memory costs and constrained supplies as continuing pressure on handset makers and buyers. The downturn was particularly sharp in the third quarter: IDC and Omdia estimated year-on-year shipment declines of 22 percent and 20 percent respectively, as rising prices and component shortages squeezed the market.

Don't expect things to get better in the short term, as Counterpoint forecasts another 6 percent drop during 2027. But it believes that the market may see a 6 percent rebound in 2028, as memory supplies improve, pricing begins to stabilize, and pent-up demand for replacement phones returns. The 2026 outlook has been shaped by sharply rising component costs, particularly for DRAM and NAND flash.

According to IDC , memory costs have risen approximately 350 percent since last year and are still climbing. As Reg readers will be well aware by now, this has been blamed on the memory manufacturers prioritizing output of high-margin chips to go into AI servers and GPU accelerators, creating a shortage of the mainstream components that are needed for devices like PCs and phones. Vendors have responded by raising prices, reducing memory configurations, and withdrawing lower-cost models.

The company says it has rotated or revoked potentially exposed credentials and that neither its apps nor its browser extensions were affected. 1Password hasn't explained how access to an employee's GitHub account enabled the attacker to deploy malicious code to its backend services, nor has it detailed the extent of the attacker's access to its software build and distribution infrastructure.
