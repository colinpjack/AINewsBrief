While a typical PC board has about 12 layers of tracks, AI server boards can have 40 to 60 layers or more, and each additional layer requires more resin, copper foil, and laminate. The supply of materials is set to be constrained through at least the first half of 2027, the report says, with meaningful relief unlikely before mid-2028. Programs to deliver PCBs for automotive, industrial, medical and even the defense industries are being crowded out as AI soaks up scarce capacity, it claims.
